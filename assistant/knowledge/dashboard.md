# Dashboard

The dashboard summarizes your orders. It needs the dashboard permission, which buyers and suppliers have.

## Indicators

Four cards at the top. Each one links to the orders list already filtered.

- **Total orders**: every order, all time.
- **Open orders**: orders that are not closed yet.
- **Waiting for you**: orders where it is your turn, meaning the current status has an action available to your role.
- **ERP failures**: orders whose submission to the ERP failed and need a retry or a fix.

## Charts

- **Orders in the last 30 days**: a bar chart of new orders per day, with the total for the period.
- **Orders by status**: how orders are distributed across statuses.

## Recent orders

A table with the latest orders. Click one to open it, or use _View all_ to go to the full list.

## Orders list

The orders list supports searching, filtering by status and by dates, and showing only orders pending on you or created
by you. Filters live in the address, so you can bookmark or share a filtered list. Columns can be sorted, and the list is paginated.
